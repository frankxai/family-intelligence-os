# Quality gates

Apply these gates before calling a substantial research report, proof summary, evidence audit, or GEDCOM review complete. A failed gate does not block useful work; it changes the completion claim and must be disclosed.

## 1. Question and scope

- The research question is atomic and answerable.
- Time, place, jurisdictions, identities, and exclusions are explicit.
- The research performed is proportionate to the conclusion claimed.
- Material inaccessible, unindexed, destroyed, paywalled, or out-of-scope sources are listed.

## 2. Provenance and citations

- Every material factual assertion points to a source or is labeled as a lead/inference.
- Citations identify the item consulted and its archival/database path.
- Derivative indexes and user trees are not described as original records.
- Missing citation fields are visible; none were invented.
- AI-generated citations, record identifiers, URLs, quotations, and transcriptions were verified against the source.

## 3. Evidence and independence

- Source, information, and evidence classifications are kept separate.
- Informants, timing, likely knowledge, and possible bias were considered per assertion.
- Shared-origin items are not counted as independent corroboration.
- Meaningful negative evidence includes a coverage and search-adequacy rationale.
- The evidence ledger includes both supporting and challenging evidence.

## 4. Identity and chronology

- Same-name people and competing identities were tested across a whole identity pattern.
- Names, places, dates, calendars, and jurisdictions retain their recorded form alongside normalization.
- Timelines are chronologically possible, or anomalies are explained.
- Relationships are not inferred solely from household order, surname, tree placement, or DNA prediction.

## 5. Conflict and conclusion

- Every material conflict is resolved or clearly remains unresolved.
- The strongest alternative hypothesis is addressed fairly.
- The conclusion status matches the evidence and research scope.
- The reasoning shows how evidence supports the conclusion instead of merely listing records.
- The report states what new evidence could revise the conclusion.

## 6. Privacy, rights, and representation

- Living people, minors, DNA, sensitive relationships, and private contributors were screened.
- Distribution and consent scope are stated.
- Record, image, and media rights are respected.
- Community-restricted knowledge and authority protocols are honored.
- AI-modified images are labeled, cited, preserved with originals, and used only as illustrations.
- Material AI assistance is disclosed when context requires it.

## 7. Reproducibility and handoff

- Search logs contain collection coverage, query variants or image ranges, dates, outcomes, and negative results.
- Stable claim and evidence identifiers connect the plan, ledger, report, and files.
- Originals were preserved; transformations have a diff or audit note and rollback path.
- Highest-value next actions are ordered by their ability to change the conclusion.

## Verdict

Use one:

- `complete_for_scope`: all applicable gates pass for the stated question and scope.
- `provisional`: useful conclusion, but one or more named gates remain open.
- `research_lead`: evidence is directional and not yet sufficient for a conclusion.
- `blocked_by_access`: the next material test requires unavailable records, permission, credentials, payment, or in-person work.
- `human_review_required`: privacy, identity merge, publication, contact, cultural authority, or another human-only decision is pending.
