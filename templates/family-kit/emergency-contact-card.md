# Private family emergency contact card

Template only. An adult must supply and verify every field. Keep the completed card in appropriate private/offline places. The useful minimum should already be approved for its intended readers; private archive release follows a separate governed process.

- Intended readers and location: [fill in privately]
- Primary trusted contact and verified phone: [fill in privately]
- Backup trusted contact and verified phone: [fill in privately]
- Relevant local emergency/service number, verified for location: [fill in privately]
- Help instruction a child can understand: [adult-written]
- Last checked with contacts/services: [date]
- Responsible adult / next review: [private reference / date]
- Printed copy identifier and replacement log: [private reference]
- Offline location: [private instruction]

Keep passwords, recovery codes, home-access keys, full medical histories and private archive links off this card. Do not place sensitive fields on publicly visible tags. The AI may format supplied details; it must not invent phone numbers or establish emergency authority.

A QR/NFC code is an optional online supplement. The readable card must remain useful when Internet, AI, phone power or sign-in is unavailable. Printed copies cannot be remotely revoked: replace old copies and contact the people who received them.
