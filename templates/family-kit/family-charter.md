# Our family's charter

Draft template. Values are chosen by people; the AI can help clarify examples and questions. Complete this in private and name the people entitled to participate in revisions.

- Authors and steward: [private references]
- Version and review date: [version / date]
- Who may read it: [specific audience]
- What we want to practice: [three to five chosen values]
- What each value looks like in daily life: [concrete actions]
- How we ask questions and disagree: [agreed practice]
- What we keep private: [boundaries]
- How we use AI and devices together: [purpose, limits and adult role]
- How someone can ask for help: [plain language]
- What remains open for discussion: [questions]
- How a change becomes agreed: [human revision process]

Example prompt for discussion: Describe a time when you changed your mind after finding better evidence. What helped you do it?

Sharing the charter is separate from sharing private stories used to draft it. No moral/personality scores, biometric profiles or automated judgments about family members.
