# Knowledge and ontology engineering
Status: draft validator and vocabulary implemented; storage, RDF/SHACL execution and authorized projections not connected.

Use claims as first-class entities. A relationship assertion is a claim with candidate subject/object, predicate, evidence, temporal extent and uncertainty, not an unqualified edge asserting parentage.
People, artifacts, testimony, claims, principles, lessons and collections remain distinct. The TypeScript draft graph deliberately cannot admit accepted state.

The accompanying vocabulary maps artifacts/testimony/claims to PROV entities, extraction/review to activities and accountable actors to agents. It is a vocabulary, not a standards certification or an operating inference engine. TypeScript validation enforces structural draft boundaries. It does not establish consent, legal authority, source authenticity or access rights.

Permission-scoped retrieval: resolve subject and tenant → evaluate scope and purpose → build eligible resource/source IDs → retrieve over eligible chunks → re-check source/derivative revocation → generate with citations → validate bounded output → human review. Filter before vector search, not after generated answers.
Search and graph databases are derived indexes; PostgreSQL authority and the family gateway remain canonical. Propagate withdrawal to materialized paths, embeddings, summaries and caches.

Keep family principles authored/versioned. Do not infer universal agreement from a founder's statement or merge theological belief into historical evidence.
Use machine-readable opaque IDs. Public graphs are separately approved projections and omit prohibited nodes, edges and source metadata entirely.
