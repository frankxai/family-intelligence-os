# Capture contract
Candidate records require tenant, owner, type, sensitivity, scope, consent reference, source IDs, source page/timecode, observed date and draft state. An unknown or missing field is unresolved, not a default grant.
Scanned books: originals → isolated inspection → rights and consent check → OCR derivative with page mapping and confidence → approved scoped chunks → evidence-linked notes → human curation. Low-confidence handwriting stays unresolved.
Treat a partial scan as a field note; do not claim to summarize unseen chapters. Keep full scans private. Publication requires separate rights and living-person review.
Graph edges never confer access. A scoped projection must check node, edge, source and derivative permissions. Revocation suppresses dependent indexes, excerpts, summaries and cached model context.
